## Common questions

**How many people can share a room?**
Exactly two. Shorty is built specifically for one-on-one roommate pairs, not group living situations.

**My invite link isn't working.**
Make sure the person you're inviting already has Shorty installed and open at least once before tapping the link, and that you're sending them the most recent link you generated (an old one from a previous attempt won't work if you left and recreated the room since).

**Where is my data stored?**
Entirely in yours and your roommate's own iCloud accounts. Shorty has no backend server or database of its own — see the [Privacy Policy](https://fdonoghue87-code.github.io/Shorty/PRIVACY.html) for details.

**How do I cancel Shorty Plus?**
Shorty Plus is billed through Apple's App Store, so it's managed the same way as any other subscription: on your iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

**I want to stop sharing a room.**
In the app, go to Settings → Leave This Room.
